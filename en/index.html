Location and trips

Surrounded by nature, yet central

The apartment is on Kurhausstraße in Bad Harzburg, right at the edge of the forest. Restaurants are within walking distance and the Harz National Park starts just behind the house.

Things to do

  • Burgberg hill with cable car
  • Treetop walk and suspended forest railway
  • Radau waterfall and Rabenklippen cliffs
  • Brine spa and spa park
  • Goslar with its imperial palace and old town
  • Hiking, and skiing in winter
